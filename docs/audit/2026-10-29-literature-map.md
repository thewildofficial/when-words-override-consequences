# Literature map for the 2026-10-29 sprint (review draft)

Reading depth labels: ABS = abstract only; PART = abstract plus the first ~50 KB of the HTML text (the fetch tool truncates there, so later sections, appendices and some results are unread); FULL = read end to end in an earlier run. Nothing here is a novelty claim. Search was a handful of web queries, not a systematic review.

| Paper | Depth | What it tested (as read) | Bearing on us |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2605.00226 Broken Links Between Observations, Beliefs, Actions (Sobotka et al.) https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.00226 | PART | Open models (Llama 3.1, Qwen3, gpt-oss) in incomplete-information games incl. generalized Kuhn poker. Probes recover latent state better than verbal reports; beliefs degrade with multi-hop reasoning; steering toward beliefs converts to action weakly; neither belief-conditioning consistently improves payoff. Searching the read portion for expected value / action value / Q-value found only payoff-matrix and "expected difference in payoffs between conditioning modes" mentions. | Belief probing and steering in Kuhn are prior art. Action-value decoding was not seen in the read part (not proof of absence). |
| 2609.07478 Internal Anatomy of Strategic Choice (Ferraz, Houf, Ferrea) https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07478 | PART | 4 open models, one-shot ordinal 2x2 games with visible payoffs. Decodes the expected-payoff advantage of the canonical action vs a uniform opponent ("incentive gap") and the eventual choice from activations; tests availability, recruitment beyond the objective incentive, and susceptibility to intervention along the incentive direction (abstract: "where tested, whether strengthening it shifted preference"); base vs instruct pair differ in whether incentive reaches choice. Opponent's incentive not decodable above chance. | Closest overlap with our EV-causal-use question. It covers visible-payoff, one-shot, ordinal games. It does not (as read) cover hidden-state posterior-weighted EV, observed-opponent-action conditioning, or Kuhn poker. Update after reading the intervention passages in the first ~50 KB: it strengthens the incentive signal directly with the game and prompt fixed, reports effects selective by game family, and states that same-norm random-direction effects were close to zero in the Qwen models. Dose-zero baseline preference varied from 0.41 to 0.97 across families and models. Later sections remain unread. |
| 2606.17056 The Value Axis (Jiang, Kauvar, Lindsey) https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17056 | ABS | Trajectory-level "value" axis for Qwen3-8B built from synthetic in-context RL data; steering toward high value suppresses self-correction. | Value is expected goal success of a trajectory, not action-value in a game. Shows value-like directions can be causal. |
| 2607.19369 Composition-Bounded Predictive States in Poker Autoregressive Models https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19369 | PART (abstract, intro) | No-range Limit Hold'em transformer trained on action and value targets. Opponent-range probes mostly explained by visible betting composition; matched-composition comparisons negative in every seed. | Any decoded-EV probe needs a public-history composition baseline. |

| 2609.18080 shared/probe-only/random features | FULL (earlier run) | Five-seed flip rates 0.105 / 0.059 / 0.010 for shared / probe-only / random features; cumulative ablation peaks 0.31 at N=96. | Random-feature baselines are necessary controls. |

## What this changes
- The H3 (Kuhn EV causal use) novelty slice is narrower than I proposed: visible-payoff EV decoding with intervention and a same-norm random-direction control exists (2609.07478, intervention passages read). What remains unaddressed in the read material is posterior-weighted EV that requires inferring a hidden card from an observed opponent action, in a setting with solver ground truth, with composition and position controls. Not established as novel; needs the unread parts of 2609.07478 and a wider search.
- J-lens: the literature supplies the method, not an independent validity test of editing at low KL. The repo's own 1-nat limit is the only criterion used here.
