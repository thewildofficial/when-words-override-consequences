# Y4 exploratory probe (est. ~$0.3; 480 Y3 prompts, plain rendering, last-prompt-token residual at layers 0,4,..,64; raw features kept off-repo, 60 MB)
Method: kernel-ridge linear probe (numpy, inner 3-fold alpha), 5-fold CV grouped by item id (all four conditions share items, groups split per condition) or grouped by outs value (held-out outs counts); AUC reported because sign-accuracy is biased below 0.5 by fold class imbalance (layer 0 gave 0.23-0.30 accuracy, a CV artifact; analysis.txt is superseded by analysis_auc.txt). Controls: shuffled-label probe at L40 (AUC 0.33-0.55, mean ~0.42), raw-numbers-only baseline.
Findings (all exploratory, n=120 per condition, 5120-d features, no CIs):
- The raw numbers (outs, unseen, pot, bet and logs) predict the Call/Fold label with AUC 1.0 in every condition and both groupings. So the label is fully determined by the input numbers; any probe success is compatible with echoing input numbers and no computed belief.
- Call/Fold decodability by item: D3 (equity derived from outs, behaviour 60% always-Call): AUC 0.99 (L40), 0.88-0.95 (L48-64). D4: 0.81-0.90 (L40-64). D1: 1.0 (L40) but 0.69-0.77 at L32/L56. D2: 0.81-0.89. Mid layers (16-32) are at chance (0.44-0.52) for D3/D4.
- With held-out outs counts: D3 stays high (0.94-0.97 at L40-64), but D4 falls to 0.27-0.48 (below the shuffled control) and D1/D2 are erratic and often far below chance (0.22-0.37) at some layers. Below-chance AUCs under distribution shift mean the probe direction is not stable, so the D3 byouts result is not a reliable positive.
- Letter confound: the model answers with a letter. "Chose A" is decodable from late layers in D1 (0.83-0.85) and D4 (0.91-0.94 at L56-64), so late-layer signal is partly output-letter coding.
- Transfer D1+D2 -> D3+D4 (sign accuracy, optimistic bias noted): 0.50-0.65 across layers, mostly near chance; D2->D4 0.63-0.83 at some layers; D3->D4 and D4->D3 inconsistent.
Reading: no clean evidence for an internally represented win probability that behaviour fails to use. The only candidate lead (D3, held-out outs, L40-64) is not matched by D4 or by the competent conditions, and the stable control (raw numbers = perfect) means numbers-echo cannot be excluded. Decodability is not causal use; no intervention was run. A proper test needs: numeric target (equity) with number-of-digits/lexical controls, matched prompt templates, answer-letter-orthogonal labels, and more items.
