Items: Y2 (C1-C4, 480 prompts) + Y3 (D1-D4, 480 prompts), the same balanced items. Instruct model: chat template, thinking off, A/B logits after the assistant tag. Base model: raw text, question + "Answer:" and compare " A" vs " B" (prompt format differs by necessity; the trailing "Answer with a single letter." line is removed for raw).
Predictions: instruct 8B repeats the 27B pattern directionally (D1 high, D3/D4 near chance with Call bias); base model is at or below instruct on D1/D2, and its D3/D4 behaviour is at chance but may NOT be always-Call. Primary measure: per-class recall (Call vs Fold), not accuracy alone. A base/instruct difference in Call-bias on D3/D4 would be a lead about post-training, one family-size pair, no more.
